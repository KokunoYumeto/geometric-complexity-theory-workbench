# Geometric complexity theory

Mulmuley and Sohoni's programme separates the permanent from the determinant, algebraic counterparts of NP and P,
through the representation theory of orbit closures. This workbench holds our work on that programme: its hypotheses,
obstructions, plethysm and Kronecker coefficients, and border rank.

**Every result here was produced by AI (GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT Pro mode, and Claude; each record names the model, its effort and the date), and has been checked only by AI unless a record names a human who checked it.**

## Work here

| Number | Work | State |
|---|---|---|
| [WB-0002](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/mathematics-workbenches/blob/main/works/WB-0002.md) | [The hypotheses of Mulmuley's flip for permanent and determinant, after Kumar and Bürgisser–Ikenmeyer–Panova](work/WB-0002/v0.1/NOTE.md) | research note with proofs, 22 November 2026; paper in preparation |

The note shows that RH, the first hypothesis of GCT VI's theorem on explicit obstructions, is false for every
m > n ≥ 2; that PH1 on the determinant's side is false as stated; and that OH contradicts PH1's counting clauses for
m ≥ (n+1)^25. It credits what is Kumar's and what is Bürgisser, Ikenmeyer and Panova's. The literature search isn't
finished, and no priority is claimed.

## How this repository works

Each work has a permanent number and a permanent page in the [index of the workbenches](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/mathematics-workbenches). Cite that page: it
follows the work if it moves. A deposited version's folder is never edited, and its `MANIFEST.sha256` identifies its
files independently of commits. `python -I -B tools/check_workbench.py` checks the repository.
